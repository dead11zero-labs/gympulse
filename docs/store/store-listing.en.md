## Title (max. 30 characters)

GymPulse – Workout Tracker

## Short description (max. 80 characters)

Offline workout tracker for sets, plans, rest timer and body data. No account.

## Full description (max. 4000 characters)

GymPulse is a workout tracker for Android that works completely offline. Your data stays on your device, in a basement gym just as much as on the go. You don't need an account.

**Training**
- Start a workout with a plan or as a free workout
- Log sets per exercise with weight (kg) and repetitions, and tick them off
- The values from your last session appear as a reference in the "Previous" column
- Mark warm-up sets, add notes per exercise and per workout
- Rest timer with an adjustable duration
- A running workout stays visible in a notification, even when you leave the app
- A summary at the end with duration, volume and personal records

**Plans**
- Create, edit, duplicate and delete training plans
- Plan exercises with target sets and a repetition range

**Exercise library**
- Over 870 exercises for strength, stretching and cardio, with German and English names
- Search, filters by muscle group and equipment type, favorites
- Create your own exercises
- Save machines in your gym with their settings (for example seat height)
- Demonstration images for each exercise when an internet connection is available

**Dashboard and calendar**
- Weekly volume with daily bars and a comparison with the previous week
- Weekly progress against your weekly goal
- Weekly schedule: assign plans to weekdays, move or skip training days
- Week and month views with your workout history

**Body measurements**
- Record weight, body fat and circumferences
- History as a chart, with the change since your previous entry

**Back up your data**
- Export and import your data as a JSON file; you choose where it is saved

**Settings**
- Light, dark or system theme, optional Material You colors (Android 12 and newer)
- App language German or English
- Week start, weekly goal, body weight and rest duration

**Privacy**
- No account, no advertising, no sharing of your data
- The app does not send your training data to a server
- The only outgoing connection loads exercise images from GitHub. Details are in the privacy policy.

**Planned (not yet available)**
- Connection to Health Connect (write workouts, read body data), Samsung Health via Health Connect
- Optional cloud backup with Google sign-in
- CSV export, progress photos
